Murmure Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Murmure is operated by Nicolas Marquis, who is also responsible for privacy inquiries. Contact: nick.marquis@hotmail.com.

This policy explains how Murmure handles personal information when a parent or guardian creates and listens to personalized bedtime stories. It covers the app and our public support and privacy pages.

Information you provide

  • Account information: your account identifier and the email address supplied through Sign in with Apple, which may be an Apple private relay address. Your account may also include a display name and language preference. Authentication credentials and session tokens allow you to stay signed in. An encrypted Apple authorization token is retained so we can revoke access when you delete your account.
  • Family and story information: character names, relationships, descriptions, your story instructions, language and duration choices, generated avatars and covers, stories, and narrated audio. We store this content with your account to create and replay your stories.
  • Optional dictation: when you grant microphone permission and start dictation, we process your recording to turn speech into text. Typing is available instead.
  • Purchase information: product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, account identifiers, and your Story Minutes ledger. Apple handles payment details. Murmure does not receive your full payment-card information.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when you email us.

Please use nicknames or fictional characters where possible. Do not include an address, school, medical details, or other sensitive information about yourself, a child, or another person in a character description or story request. Only provide information you are authorized to share.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, save your family characters, check requests for unsuitable content, generate stories and artwork, narrate stories, transcribe optional dictation, provide playback and downloads, and deliver purchased Story Minutes. We also use relevant records to investigate failures, prevent abuse, reconcile purchases, and answer support requests.

Our infrastructure and purchase providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, device and app details, request timestamps, errors, and transaction events to operate and secure their services. Murmure does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The public support and privacy pages do not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Your AI-sharing choices

Before starting AI story processing, the app asks for permission to send character and story text to OpenAI and generated story text to Cartesia. Dictation has a separate permission before microphone audio is sent for transcription. You can decline either choice and still listen to existing stories and Murmure Originals.

Open Settings > Privacy and AI to review or withdraw these permissions. Choices are stored on your device for the signed-in account and the consent version; they are not automatically shared with your other devices or accounts. Turning a permission off blocks new requests from that device. It does not recall data already sent, delete provider records, or stop a story generation already running on the server. Account deletion and other privacy requests are described below.

AI processing and service providers

Creating a character or personalized story involves sending content to external services. AI processing is not performed entirely on your device.

  • Supabase provides account authentication, the application database, server functions, and file storage. It processes account information, family and story records, generated files, and operational requests. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI receives the text needed to check content and generate stories, character avatars, and covers. This can include the names and descriptions you enter and your story instructions. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Cartesia receives story text to create narrated audio. If you use dictation, Cartesia also receives the microphone audio needed for transcription. See Cartesia's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat processes your account identifier and purchase-related information to validate and attribute transactions and help deliver your Story Minutes. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and in-app purchase checkout and processes the information required for those services under Apple's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts our public support and privacy pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

These providers handle information under their applicable service terms and privacy practices. This policy does not promise that all provider-held records are deleted immediately or that providers have identical retention practices.

Service providers may process information outside Quebec or Canada, including in the United States. Privacy laws and government access rules in those places may differ from those where you live.

Dictation and microphone permission

Microphone access is optional and is used only when you start dictation. English dictation streams audio directly to Cartesia for transcription. French dictation uses a temporary recording that passes through our server function to Cartesia. Our transcription function holds the clip in memory for the request and does not save it to our file-storage service. The app attempts to remove temporary recording files when transcription finishes or recording is cancelled.

The resulting transcript appears in an editable text field. If you submit it, it is handled like typed family or story content. You can disable microphone access in iPhone Settings and continue typing. Provider-side retention is governed by the provider's policies.

Storage, links, and security

Account and story database access is restricted to the account owner through authentication and database access controls. We use HTTPS for service connections and restrict administrative credentials to server-side services.

Generated avatars, covers, and story audio are currently delivered through publicly accessible storage URLs. Anyone who obtains one of these URLs may be able to access the corresponding file without signing in. These files are not protected by account authentication at the file URL. Avoid putting sensitive or identifying details into content you generate or sharing file links you want to keep to yourself.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you believe your account or a file link has been accessed without your permission.

Retention and deletion

We retain account, family, story, and purchase-balance information while your account remains open so the app can provide those features. Removing a character from the active family does not necessarily erase its details from existing stories. You can delete completed or failed stories through the app. A story that is still being generated must finish or fail before it can be deleted.

To delete your account, open Settings > Delete account in Murmure and confirm. The deletion process removes your stored account and family records, stories, and associated generated files from our active application systems. For Sign in with Apple accounts, it also revokes the stored Apple authorization; reauthentication may be required to complete that step. Email us if you cannot access the app or deletion fails.

Account deletion also removes unused Story Minutes. They cannot be recovered by creating another account. Apple's refund process and any rights required by law still apply.

Deletion from active systems does not necessarily remove information immediately from infrastructure backups, operational or security logs, purchase-provider records, or communications we must retain for legal obligations or to resolve a dispute. These records follow the relevant provider's retention processes and any applicable legal requirements. We cannot recall copies that someone else has saved from a shared file link.

Downloaded audio, playback information, and app settings may also be stored on your device. Remove downloads in the app or uninstall Murmure to remove its local files. Do not assume that deleting an account remotely also removes copies already downloaded to a device.

Parents, guardians, and children

Murmure is intended for parents and guardians to operate, not for children to create independent accounts or submit their own personal information. An adult is responsible for selecting what information to enter and for supervising use of generated stories. If you believe a child submitted personal information without appropriate adult involvement, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit family information, choose fictional characters, type instead of dictate, revoke microphone permission, delete stories, and request account deletion. You can stop further AI processing by not using generation or dictation features; this does not remove information already processed.

To request access to or correction of your personal information, ask about deletion or applicable portability rights, withdraw consent to processing, or make a privacy complaint, email nick.marquis@hotmail.com. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account. Some requests may prevent us from continuing to provide features that depend on that information. Your rights and any exceptions depend on applicable law. You may also contact the privacy regulator in your jurisdiction, including Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Significant changes affecting how personal information is handled will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel as required by applicable law.
